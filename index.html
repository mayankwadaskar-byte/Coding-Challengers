FAQ

      
Questions people ask

      

      
What is Coding Challengers?
Coding Challengers runs online hackathons for college students. Each hackathon, such as CodeQuest, has its own name, theme and timeline, and you register for the one you want.

      
Who can take part?
Any college student. You can enter alone or in a team of up to four, and teams can include students from different colleges.

      
Do I need to be an expert?
No. Judges score what you built against the rubric, and a small project that works well scores better than a big one that does not run.

      
Can we use AI tools and existing libraries?
Yes, as long as you say what you used in your README. The code and the idea you submit must be your team's own work, and using other people's work without credit leads to disqualification.

      
Do we need to start from scratch?
Your repository's commit history must start in the build period. You may reuse libraries and your own earlier utility code if you list it in the README.

      
Who judges the projects?
The organizer plus outside reviewers, announced on the hackathon's page before the project round opens. Everyone scores with the same public rubric.
