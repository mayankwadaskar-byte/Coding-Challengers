# Coding Challengers

**Online hackathons for college students.** Compete. Build. Get seen as a coder.

Coding Challengers is a student-run platform that hosts free, 100% online hackathons. Pick a challenge, form a team of 1 to 4, and submit a working project from wherever you are. No travel, no live pitching, and every event is scored on a public rubric.

**Website:** [codingchallengers2.vercel.app](https://codingchallengers2.vercel.app/)

---

## CodeQuest, our first hackathon

Build a working project that solves a real problem for students or local businesses.

### Tracks

1. **Local business tools:** software a small shop, clinic or coaching class could use right away
2. **Student life:** fix something that annoys students in hostels and colleges every day
3. **Open track:** any useful project that fits neither of the above, including AI-powered tools

### Rounds and dates (all in IST)

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 6 Nov 2026 | Registration opens |
| **30 Nov 2026** | **Registration closes** |
| 7 Dec 2026 | Round 1 due: a one-page idea |
| 10 Dec 2026 | Round 2 shortlist announced |
| 21 Dec 2026 | Round 2 due: public GitHub repo, 2 to 3 minute demo video, README |
| 22 to 28 Dec 2026 | Judging |
| 29 Dec 2026 | Results announced |

### How projects are scored (out of 100)

| Criteria | Points |
|---|---|
| Working prototype and technical execution | 30 |
| Problem and idea quality | 20 |
| Innovation and creativity | 15 |
| Usefulness and potential impact | 15 |
| Demo video and presentation clarity | 10 |
| Code quality and README | 10 |

### Certificates

Every registered team that submits its Round 1 idea gets a participation certificate. Teams shortlisted to Round 2 that submit a project get a merit certificate, and the top teams get a winner certificate. Prize details, if any, are announced separately and are not guaranteed.
